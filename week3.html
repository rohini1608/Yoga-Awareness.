Week 3 Report

Warm-up and Basic Standing Postures

Weekly Activities

During the third week, we focused on gentle warm-up exercises and beginner-friendly standing yoga postures. We learned the importance of correct body alignment, comfortable movements and safe participation while practising basic yoga.

Day Activity Description
Day 1 Introduction to Warm-up Exercises Learned why warm-up exercises are useful before beginning yoga practice.
Day 2 Gentle Body Movements Practised simple neck, shoulder, arm and leg movements without forcing the body.
Day 3 Standing Yoga Postures Learned basic standing postures and their role in improving balance and body awareness.
Day 4 Posture and Body Alignment Understood the importance of maintaining comfortable posture and proper alignment.
Day 5 Practice and Weekly Review Reviewed the warm-up movements and standing postures learned during the week.

Learning Outcomes

  • Understood the importance of warm-up exercises before yoga.
  • Learned simple and gentle body movements.
  • Gained basic knowledge of standing yoga postures.
  • Understood the importance of balance and correct body alignment.
  • Learned to participate in yoga practice safely and comfortably.
