COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT · WEEK 03

Warm-up and Basic Standing Postures

Learning simple movements and beginner-friendly standing asanas.

About This Week

This week introduces the importance of preparing the body before yoga practice. It focuses on gentle warm-up activities, correct standing posture, balance and safe movement. Participants are encouraged to practise according to their comfort and ability.

Day 1 – Importance of Warm-up

The activity explains why the body should be prepared before beginning yoga postures. Gentle movements of the neck, shoulders, wrists, ankles and legs are introduced. Participants are advised to avoid sudden movements and stop if they experience pain or discomfort.

Day 2 – Gentle Body Movements

Simple warm-up movements are discussed and demonstrated. These may include shoulder rotations, gentle arm movements, ankle rotations and comfortable side stretches. The purpose is to improve awareness of body movement and prepare for basic postures.

Day 3 – Tadasana (Mountain Pose)

Tadasana is introduced as a basic standing posture. Participants learn to stand comfortably with their feet grounded, spine naturally upright and shoulders relaxed. The posture helps develop awareness of standing alignment and balance.

Day 4 – Vrikshasana (Tree Pose)

Vrikshasana is explained as a balance posture. Participants learn the basic idea of standing on one leg while keeping the other foot at a comfortable position, such as the ankle or lower leg. Support from a wall or chair can be used. The knee should not be pressed directly.

Day 5 – Practice Review and Discussion

The team reviews the warm-up movements and standing postures introduced during the week. Participants can discuss which movements felt comfortable and ask questions about posture and balance. The importance of patience, gradual learning and safe practice is reinforced.

Week 3 – Learning Outcomes

  • Understood the importance of warming up.
  • Learned examples of gentle preparatory movements.
  • Understood the basic steps of Tadasana.
  • Learned about balance through Vrikshasana.
  • Recognized the importance of safe movement.
