COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT · WEEK 06

Guided Practice and Community Interaction

Sharing yoga knowledge through guided activities and teamwork.

About This Week

This week focuses on organizing beginner-friendly yoga awareness activities and encouraging community participation. It emphasizes communication, teamwork, clear demonstrations and respect for participants' individual abilities.

Day 1 – Planning a Guided Yoga Session

The team prepares a simple session plan covering an introduction, gentle warm-up, selected beginner postures, breathing awareness and relaxation. Responsibilities are divided among team members. The plan considers the available space, time and comfort of participants.

Day 2 – Demonstration of Basic Postures

The team reviews how to explain basic postures such as Tadasana and comfortable seated positions. Demonstrations are planned using simple instructions and clear movements. Participants are encouraged to observe first and practise only when they feel comfortable.

Day 3 – Community Interaction

Community interaction provides an opportunity to explain the purpose of yoga awareness and answer general questions. Team members use simple language and encourage participants to share their experiences. The activity also helps identify topics that may need further explanation.

Day 4 – Guided Beginner Practice

A guided beginner practice may include gentle warm-up movements, comfortable standing or seated postures and a short relaxation activity. The session should move at a suitable pace, with alternatives for participants who cannot perform a particular movement.

Day 5 – Team Review and Participant Discussion

The team reviews the session plan and any community interaction carried out during the week. Questions, suggestions and observations are recorded. Team members discuss how to improve explanations, participation and safety in future activities.

Week 6 – Learning Outcomes

  • Improved teamwork and activity planning.
  • Learned to explain basic yoga movements clearly.
  • Understood the importance of community interaction.
  • Recognized the need for inclusive practice.
  • Identified ways to improve guided sessions.
