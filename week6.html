COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT

Week 6 Report

Guided Yoga Practice and Community Interaction

Weekly Activities

During the sixth week, we focused on guided yoga practice and interaction with community members. We discussed the importance of practising yoga regularly and explained simple yoga movements to beginners. We also encouraged participants to share their questions and experiences. Teamwork, communication and inclusive participation were important parts of this week's activities.

Day Activity Description
Day 1 Planning a Guided Yoga Session Discussed how to organize a simple yoga session suitable for beginners.
Day 2 Demonstration of Basic Yoga Revised basic warm-up movements and simple yoga postures through demonstration.
Day 3 Guided Yoga Practice Practised beginner-friendly yoga movements with attention to comfort and safety.
Day 4 Community Interaction Interacted with participants, listened to their questions and discussed yoga awareness.
Day 5 Teamwork and Weekly Review Reviewed the week's activities and discussed the importance of teamwork and communication.

Learning Outcomes

  • Understood how to plan a simple guided yoga session.
  • Improved knowledge of demonstrating basic yoga practices.
  • Learned the importance of safe and comfortable participation.
  • Improved communication through community interaction.
  • Developed teamwork and coordination skills.
