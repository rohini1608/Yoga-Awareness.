COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT · WEEK 05

Breathing Practices and Mindfulness

Developing awareness of natural breathing and attention.

About This Week

This week focuses on breathing awareness and simple mindfulness activities. Participants learn the importance of comfortable, natural breathing and paying attention to the present moment. The activities are introductory and do not involve forceful breath control.

Day 1 – Introduction to Breathing Awareness

The activity explains the role of breathing in everyday life and introduces the idea of observing the natural breath. Participants sit or rest comfortably and notice the movement of breathing without trying to change it. The importance of avoiding strain is explained.

Day 2 – Diaphragmatic Breathing Awareness

Participants are introduced to gentle belly-breath awareness while sitting comfortably or lying down. They can observe the abdomen moving naturally with each breath. The practice remains relaxed, without holding the breath or attempting to breathe deeply by force.

Day 3 – Introduction to Pranayama

The meaning of pranayama is introduced as a traditional area of yoga involving breath regulation. The team discusses the importance of learning breathing techniques gradually under qualified guidance. Beginners are encouraged to start with comfortable natural breathing.

Day 4 – Introduction to Mindfulness

Mindfulness is explained as paying attention to the present moment. A simple activity involves noticing the breath, sounds and body sensations while sitting comfortably. Participants are reminded that wandering thoughts are normal and attention can gently return to the activity.

Day 5 – Breathing Awareness Review

The team reviews the breathing awareness activities and discusses how participants felt during the exercises. The importance of calm, natural breathing and regular relaxation breaks is highlighted. Any discomfort should be a reason to stop the activity.

Week 5 – Learning Outcomes

  • Understood the meaning of breathing awareness.
  • Learned to observe natural breathing comfortably.
  • Received an introduction to pranayama.
  • Understood the basic idea of mindfulness.
  • Recognized the need to avoid forceful breathing practices.
