COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT · WEEK 04

Seated and Relaxation Postures

Understanding comfortable sitting, gentle stretches and relaxation.

About This Week

This week introduces selected seated yoga positions and relaxation practices. The activities focus on comfortable posture, gentle movement and awareness of the body. Participants are encouraged to use suitable support and avoid forcing any position.

Day 1 – Comfortable Sitting Positions

The team explains the importance of sitting comfortably during yoga practice. Participants learn about keeping the back naturally upright, relaxing the shoulders and avoiding unnecessary strain. Chairs or cushions may be used when sitting on the floor is uncomfortable.

Day 2 – Sukhasana (Easy Pose)

Sukhasana is introduced as a simple cross-legged sitting position for people who find it comfortable. The activity explains how to sit with support, relax the shoulders and maintain a natural spine position. Participants are reminded that a chair is an acceptable alternative.

Day 3 – Gentle Seated Stretches

Simple seated stretches are explained, including gentle shoulder movements and comfortable side stretches. The movements are performed slowly without bouncing or forcing the body. Participants are encouraged to maintain normal breathing throughout the activity.

Day 4 – Balasana (Child's Pose)

Balasana is introduced as a resting posture that some practitioners may find comfortable. The activity explains the basic position and the use of cushions for support. Participants who experience knee, back or other discomfort should choose a different resting position.

Day 5 – Relaxation and Weekly Review

The week concludes with a discussion about relaxation and body awareness. Participants learn about resting in a comfortable position, releasing unnecessary muscle tension and observing natural breathing. The team reviews the postures and records important learning points.

Week 4 – Learning Outcomes

  • Understood the importance of comfortable sitting.
  • Learned the basic idea of Sukhasana.
  • Explored gentle seated stretches.
  • Understood a supported relaxation posture.
  • Recognized the importance of adapting postures to individual comfort.
