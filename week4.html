Week 4 Report

Seated Yoga Postures and Relaxation

Weekly Activities

During the fourth week, we focused on simple seated yoga postures, gentle stretching and relaxation techniques. We learned how to sit comfortably, maintain body awareness and perform basic movements without putting unnecessary pressure on the body.

Day Activity Description
Day 1 Introduction to Seated Postures Learned about comfortable sitting positions and their importance during yoga practice.
Day 2 Simple Seated Stretching Practised gentle seated stretches for the shoulders, arms and back.
Day 3 Basic Seated Yoga Practices Learned beginner-friendly seated postures while focusing on balance and comfort.
Day 4 Relaxation Techniques Understood the importance of relaxing the body and avoiding unnecessary muscle tension.
Day 5 Practice and Weekly Review Reviewed the seated postures and relaxation methods learned during the week.

Learning Outcomes

  • Understood the importance of comfortable sitting positions.
  • Learned simple seated stretching exercises.
  • Gained basic knowledge of seated yoga postures.
  • Understood the value of relaxation and body awareness.
  • Learned to practise movements according to individual comfort.
