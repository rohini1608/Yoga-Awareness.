COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT

Week 7 Report

Yoga Awareness and Feedback

Weekly Activities

During the seventh week, we focused on spreading awareness about the importance of yoga and collecting feedback from community participants. We reviewed the yoga practices learned in previous weeks and discussed how simple yoga activities can be included in daily routines. Feedback and suggestions helped us understand the participants' experiences and learning needs.

Day Activity Description
Day 1 Yoga Awareness Discussion Discussed the importance of yoga and encouraged awareness of regular practice.
Day 2 Revision of Yoga Practices Revised basic postures, warm-up exercises and breathing practices learned earlier.
Day 3 Community Feedback Collected participants' opinions and suggestions about the yoga awareness activities.
Day 4 Yoga in Daily Routine Discussed simple ways to continue suitable yoga and relaxation practices in everyday life.
Day 5 Feedback Review and Weekly Summary Reviewed the feedback and summarized the important points learned during the week.

Learning Outcomes

  • Improved understanding of yoga awareness in the community.
  • Revised basic yoga postures and breathing practices.
  • Learned the importance of collecting and reviewing feedback.
  • Understood how yoga can be included in daily routines.
  • Improved communication and community engagement skills.
