COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT · WEEK 07

Feedback and Follow-up Awareness

Reviewing community understanding and encouraging continued learning.

About This Week

This week focuses on reviewing the awareness activities and collecting feedback from participants where possible. It helps the team understand which topics were useful, what questions remain and how yoga awareness can be encouraged beyond the project sessions.

Day 1 – Review of Previous Activities

The team reviews the topics introduced during the earlier weeks, including yoga basics, standing postures, seated postures and breathing awareness. The purpose is to identify the main learning points and prepare a simple revision plan for community members.

Day 2 – Collecting Participant Feedback

Participants may be invited to share their opinions about the awareness activities. Simple questions can cover which topics they understood, which demonstrations were helpful and what they would like to learn next. Responses should be recorded accurately and respectfully.

Day 3 – Identifying Learning Difficulties

The team considers common difficulties that beginners may face, such as lack of time, unfamiliarity with postures or discomfort while sitting on the floor. Possible solutions include shorter activities, chair-based alternatives, clearer explanations and guidance from trained instructors.

Day 4 – Encouraging Regular Healthy Habits

The activity discusses realistic ways to maintain healthy habits. Examples include setting aside a suitable time for gentle movement, taking relaxation breaks and practising natural breathing awareness. Participants are encouraged to choose activities that are appropriate for their needs.

Day 5 – Feedback Review and Follow-up Plan

The team organizes the feedback and observations collected during the week. Important suggestions are identified and a follow-up awareness plan is prepared. The team also reflects on how communication and demonstrations can be improved.

Week 7 – Learning Outcomes

  • Understood the importance of participant feedback.
  • Identified common difficulties faced by beginners.
  • Learned ways to improve yoga awareness activities.
  • Recognized the value of regular healthy habits.
  • Prepared ideas for follow-up awareness.
